## v2.5.1 — Newer, cheaper Sonnet

**Claude Sonnet 5 replaces Sonnet 4.6** as the premium bundled-AI option in Settings → AI. It's the newer generation and costs less per token. Anyone who had Sonnet 4.6 selected is moved to Sonnet 5 automatically. Claude Haiku 4.5 remains the default for everyone.

**Own-key users** now see Claude Opus 5 in place of Opus 4.8.

Everything from v2.5.0 (bundled AI, live transcription, calendar Join, crash-proof recording) is unchanged.
